OUR STORY

Little beginnings.
Lasting confidence.

Little Steps Public School is a demonstration school brand built around one simple idea: every child deserves a joyful, supportive start.

Our learning approach blends foundational skills with creativity, play, movement and social-emotional growth. We want children to leave each day feeling proud of what they tried, not just what they got right.

We work in partnership with families, keeping communication open and celebrating each learner's progress along the way.
