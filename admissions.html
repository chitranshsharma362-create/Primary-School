Good to know.

Which classes are included? +

This demo school lists Nursery, LKG, UKG and Classes 1–5. A real school should update the classes it actually offers.

What documents may be required? +

The school may request a birth certificate, previous school records (where applicable), photographs and parent/guardian identification. Confirm the exact list with the school office.

Does submitting this form confirm admission? +

No. This is an enquiry only. Admission is subject to the school's actual eligibility criteria, seat availability and process.
