OUR LEARNING JOURNEY

Every age has its own magic.

We build strong foundations while giving children room to explore ideas and find their voice.

01
☀PLAY · EXPLORE · DISCOVER
AGES 3–5 · PRE-PRIMARY

First discoveries

A gentle introduction to school through play, stories, songs, sensory exploration and guided routines.

  • Language and early literacy
  • Number sense and patterns
  • Fine motor skills and creative play
  • Social confidence and independence
02
✎READ · WRITE · BUILD
CLASSES 1–2 · FOUNDATIONS

Finding their feet

Children strengthen core skills through structured lessons, stories, practical tasks and collaborative activities.

  • Reading fluency and writing
  • Mathematical thinking
  • Environmental awareness
  • Art, movement and communication
03
✦QUESTION · CREATE · GROW
CLASSES 3–5 · PRIMARY

Growing independent minds

Project work and inquiry encourage learners to connect concepts, solve problems and work together.

  • Language, mathematics and EVS
  • Science and real-world discovery
  • Digital awareness and research basics
  • Teamwork, presentations and projects
